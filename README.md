## A problem I've noticed and want to solve
One challenge I’ve observed is that students may be provided with plenty of opportunities to learn, but they fail to use those resources in a systematic way. There’s an endless number of tutorials, courses, roadmaps, and other materials that are available; however, it is hard to determine what should be learned, when and how.

I want to investigate the possibility of providing a more structured way of learning technology for students who just started their path in programming.

## Something I learned outside a classroom
Of the many lessons I have learned beyond school walls, one of the most important is that comprehending something is worlds apart from merely knowing how to do it.

When working on programming assignments and problem-solving, there have been numerous times where I ran into problems that could not be resolved by merely remembering the syntax. I had to look up information in documentation, research the cause of the problem, try out different methods, and even rethink the entire approach.

This made me realize how important it is to feel comfortable being stumped and viewing problems as something to solve.
